Research Profile

About

Global Systems Risk Analysis examines how geopolitical developments interact with global economic systems and strategic industries. The focus is on understanding how political shocks, security dynamics, and macroeconomic disruption reshape the environment in which multinational companies and financial institutions operate.

Particular attention is given to the transmission channels through which geopolitical change propagates across the global economy and how these dynamics translate into strategic risk for internationally exposed organizations.

The platform combines geopolitical analysis and international political economy with business and consulting acumen to provide concise assessments relevant to corporate strategy, enterprise risk management, and long‑term investment decisions.

The objective is to connect geopolitical analysis with the strategic concerns of companies, financial institutions, and decision-makers operating across an increasingly fragmented global environment.

Analytical Focus

Research themes currently include:

  • Geopolitical shifts and macroeconomic disruption affecting global markets
  • Energy systems, logistics networks, and strategic supply-chain risk
  • Technology competition, industrial policy, and innovation ecosystems
  • Strategic implications for banking, financial institutions, and multinational companies
  • Scenario-based assessments

Mission

To assess how geopolitical and macroeconomic developments shape global economic systems and to translate those developments into structured insights relevant to strategic risk, enterprise risk, and corporate strategy.

Based in Barcelona. Publishing short analytical briefings and selected commentary on LinkedIn.
